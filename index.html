FRAUNCES

Fraunces is a display, "Old Style" soft-serif typeface inspired by the mannerisms of early 20th century typefaces such as Windsor, Souvenir, and the Cooper Series. Fraunces was designed by Phaedra Charles and Flavia Zimbardi, partners at Undercase Type.Fraunces is a Variable Font with four axes: Weight (wght), Optical Size (opsz), Softness (SOFT), and Wonky (WONK). The Softness axis controls the “wetness” or “inkiness” of the typeface. The Wonky axis controls the manual substitution of “wonky” characters, such as the lean of the h, n, and m glyphs in the Roman, and the flagged ball terminals of the b, d, h, k, l, v, and w glyphs of the Italic.

BODY COPY

Jazz is a genre of music built around unique rhythms and improvisation. One of the key aspects of jazz is improvisation, where musicians create different melodies as they perform. Jazz music is similar to a conversation between people because it implements a call and response, where musicians listen and react to each other musically. This way, musicians can add individuality to their performance but also blend within the ensemble.

Jazz originated in New Orleans, Louisiana in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. Jazz was heavily influenced by African rhythms, the blues, and ragtime music. Jazz later spread to major cities such as Chicago and New York and was widespread by the 1920’s. As jazz music continued to evolve, many different styles developed including bebop, swing, and smooth jazz.

Some key jazz musicians are Louis Armstrong, Miles Davis, Ella Fitzgerald, and John Coltrane. Each of these musicians helped shape Jazz in different ways. Louis Armstrong became famous for his trumpet playing and improvisation. Duke Ellington was known as a great composer and band leader. Ella Fitzgerald is regarded as one of the best jazz vocalists of all time, known for her scat style singing. Lastly, Miles Davis was a trumpeter and a pioneer of new styles of jazz. These musicians had a major influence on jazz and helped the genre continue to grow and change.
